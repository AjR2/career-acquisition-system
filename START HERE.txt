CAS: Career Acquisition System
==============================

CAS watches employers' job boards for you, filters jobs by your rules (pay, location, titles),
drafts a tailored resume for the best matches using only facts you confirm, and fills in the
application form. You review everything and click submit yourself. Nothing is sent without you.

You need: a Mac, an internet connection, and an Anthropic API key (CAS walks you through it;
expect about $1 a week).

TO START
1. Double-click "Start CAS".
   - If your Mac says it "cannot be opened" or "cannot verify the developer": click Done/Cancel,
     open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
     "Start CAS". Then double-click it again. (Older macOS: right-click "Start CAS" > Open.)
   - If you do not have Node.js yet, CAS opens its download page. Install it, then double-click
     "Start CAS" again.
   - The first start installs everything (about 5 minutes).
2. Your browser opens the "Get started" page. Follow the steps from top to bottom.
3. Keep the Terminal window open while you use CAS. Close it to stop.

EVERY DAY
Double-click "Start CAS", open the Queue, and review the jobs CAS prepared. If you turned on
daily search, CAS searches each morning at 6:30 by itself while your Mac is on.

The starter employer list is 48 healthcare employers that post jobs on job boards CAS can read
(Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby). Most hospitals and large health systems use other systems (such as
Workday) that CAS cannot read; search those directly.
